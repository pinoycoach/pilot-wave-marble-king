## How to use this file

Each pair shows the same content twice: the careful-machine version, then his version. Do not study the AFTER lines as templates to copy. Study the *distance* between BEFORE and AFTER. The Roughening Pass is that distance, applied by subtraction.

---

## PAIR 1: Over-explanation

**BEFORE (careful machine):**
> I found an edge on the photograph. It was not a scratch. It was something more intentional than that, a deliberate mark that someone had made on purpose, which suggested the damage carried meaning.

**AFTER (his):**
> An edge. Not a scratch. An edge.

**What was subtracted:** every word that told the reader what the previous words meant. The repetition does the work the explanation was failing to do. Three sentences, six words, and the reader supplies "intentional" themselves, which is why they believe it.

---

## PAIR 2: The resolved ending, and the adjective

**BEFORE (careful machine):**
> Losing my mother taught me that grief never really leaves, but I have learned to live with it. I was devastated for a long time. In the end, that acceptance is what healing looks like.

**AFTER (his):**
> PhiPhi is asleep on the chair my mother used to sit in. I have not moved the chair.

**What was subtracted:** the lesson, the reassurance, and the adjective. "Devastated" became a chair. The ending does not resolve; it just won't move the chair. This is Move 8 (the object carries the feeling) and Roughening tell #3 (the resolved ending) in one cut.

## PAIR 3: Smooth transitions and clean parallels
*(Constructed demonstration. Replace with a real pair when one surfaces.)*

**BEFORE (careful machine):**
> Moreover, the price of LPG increased by eleven pesos this morning. This increase matters because it affects not only households but also the small businesses that depend on it. As such, the burden falls hardest on those least able to bear it.

**AFTER (his):**
> LPG went up eleven pesos this morning. The new price is taped over the old one.
>
> The carinderia two streets down still opened.

**What was subtracted:** "Moreover," "As such," the explained significance, the balanced not-only-but-also. What remains is a fact, an object, and an unbridged jump to a second fact. The reader builds the bridge. That is the iceberg working.

## The standing test

Read the BEFORE versions aloud, then the AFTER versions. The BEFORE versions sound like someone being careful. The AFTER versions sound like someone who has decided. The Roughening Pass exists to close that gap by subtraction, and this file exists so the gap is heard, not described.
